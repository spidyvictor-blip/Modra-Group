The Modra model

Everything your property needs,
under one roof.

"Real estate, done right, can leave everything it touches better than it found it." That's the whole business plan.

Most property companies do one thing. Modra is built as three that work as one: care that keeps a property at its best, renovation that takes a house to the studs and rebuilds it with intention, and capital that structures the funding — secured lending partners for the big builds, Modra's own capital for the rest.

We serve Grand Rapids, Holland, and the surrounding West Michigan lakeshore — streets we drive every day. Every house has a second draft; we write it, fund it, and maintain it.

Each division is run by someone who answers for the outcome. Clear scopes, flat quotes, permitted work, and finishes that hold up at resale and at 6 a.m. on a Tuesday.

01 · Property Services

Ongoing upkeep, done right. Monthly care plans — cleaning, exterior upkeep, curb appeal, seasonal work — plus à-la-carte gutter, roof, and handyman visits. New Modra homeowners get a month of Essential Care on us.

02 · Modra Properties

Taken to the studs. Rebuilt with intention. Full renovations led by a licensed Michigan Residential Builder — permits pulled, trades vetted, before-and-after documented.

03 · Modra Capital

How projects are funded: conservative underwriting, secured lending partners on every large project, and Modra's own capital for the small and swift. Structured before the first swing of a hammer.

Why people trust Modra

Owner-led accountability

Straight answers

Good questions, honest answers.

No fine print, no runaround. If you're wondering it, someone else asked it first.

What areas do you serve?

Grand Rapids, Holland, and the surrounding West Michigan lakeshore. If you're near the edge of that, ask — we'd rather tell you honestly than stretch.

How does billing work on care plans?

Monthly plans with a three-month minimum, then month-to-month. First visits run deeper — we're establishing a baseline, not just passing through.

Where's the line between upkeep and construction?

Maintenance work we handle directly. Anything requiring permits or a licensed trade runs through Modra Properties under a licensed Michigan Residential Builder — with the permit pulled and the inspection passed.

Will the quote change once you start?

The number you approve is the number you pay. If we find something genuinely unexpected behind a wall, we stop, show you, and you decide before anything else happens.

Can I sell you my house directly?

Yes. If it needs work, that's our favorite kind. No agents, no repairs, no listing hassle — we walk it, make an honest offer, and close on your timeline.

What makes Modra different?

Three divisions, one roof, and an owner who answers for each. The same people who underwrite the deal swing the hammers and come back to maintain it.
